Welcome to the Yuqian (Sophia) website!

I am an experimental materials scientist specializing in photovoltaics and spintronics, with core expertise in scalable thin-film deposition, device engineering, and data-driven process optimization. My research bridges automated experimental workflows with machine learning to accelerate materials discovery and establish composition–processing–property relationships.

I have a proven track record of translating lab-scale mechanistic insights into high-performance, stable devices and module-scale systems. Looking ahead, I aim to advance autonomous self-driving laboratories and high-throughput materials exploration for next-generation energy and quantum technologies.
