Research Program

Integrating experimental materials chemistry, device engineering, and data-driven methods to develop next-generation semiconductors for renewable energy and spin-optoelectronics.

01. Scalable Halide Photovoltaics & Module Engineering

Bridging fundamental materials synthesis with scalable module manufacturing. We elucidate Ostwald ripening-assisted crystallization dynamics, design functional inks for low-bandgap Sn/Pb alloys, and chemically modulate buried interlayers (such as phosphonic acid SAMs and ITO interfaces) to achieve high efficiency and robust operational durability.

Science 2026 Adv. Mater. 2024 Interfacial Chemistry Module Durability Flexible Devices

02. Chiral Semiconductors & Spin-Optoelectronics

Harnessing broken mirror symmetry in low-dimensional and hybrid chiral semiconductors to manipulate electron and photon angular momentum. By investigating chirality-induced spin selectivity (CISS), our work aims to develop polarization-sensitive photodetectors, circularly polarized light emitters, and novel quantum spintronic devices.

Spin-Optoelectronics CISS Effect Chiral Assembly Angular Momentum Quantum Devices

03. Data-Driven Optimization & Autonomous Laboratories

Developing machine learning frameworks (such as temporal decoupling models) to establish rigorous composition–processing–performance relationships. Moving forward, we aim to integrate robotics and automated synthesis to construct closed-loop, self-driving laboratories for the accelerated discovery of clean energy materials.
